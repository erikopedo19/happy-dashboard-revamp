- Give interactive rows, date controls, and actions iOS-style press feedback and subtle elevation without changing their actions or data.
- Keep the existing dark palette, but refine icon tiles, borders, and spacing into a more consistent iOS component system.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid endless decorative movement.

## Reports

- Tighten desktop report spacing and replace distracting continuous animation with smooth, purposeful entrances and value transitions.
